Size check

A plain 100 mm square in cut red. Drag it anywhere on the bed in the preview, so it lands on a corner of the offcut actually in the machine. It needs no design loaded — the 100 button at the top switches it on by itself, and on its own it is the whole job.

Leave at 100 until you have measured one.

Measure the cut square edge to edge and type what you got. Everything exported after that is stretched to compensate, so the millimetres on screen are the millimetres on the material. A few percent out is normal and almost always a “fit to page” or “scale to fit” setting somewhere between here and the laser — worth hunting down, but this corrects for it either way.

Colours

A traced image arrives as dozens of near-identical shades. Slide right to collect them into a few colours you can actually set.

Set every colour to

Outline colour wins over fill. Cut goes through, Score marks the surface, Engrave burns the shaded area away. Need both on one shape — burn the inside, then cut it out? Choose Cut and tick Also engrave the inside.
