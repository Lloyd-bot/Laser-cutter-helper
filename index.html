Trace

Whatever shows black above becomes the shape. Every outline closes by construction, so a traced cut line can never come out open. Works best on logos, silhouettes and bold lettering — not on photographs.

Traced shapes start as Cut. To mark the surface without going through, change it to Score or Engrave under Colours below.

Each separate piece comes out as its own shape, so you can click one in the preview and press Delete to drop it — that is how you keep the badge and lose the lettering, or the other way round. Two parts the same colour can never be told apart by the cut-off alone; crop or delete instead.

Rounding, chamfering, growing and turning at anything but a right angle bake a shape down to a plain outline, which is what a traced piece already is. Growing a shape with deep notches can tie a loop in itself — look at the preview before you cut it.

Type a size straight onto the stage, or in the fields above. Drag a selected shape to move it; arrow keys nudge by 1 mm, with Shift by 5 mm. Del removes it, Ctrl‑Z undoes. An operation set here overrides the colour it came in as.

Guides

Left behind by the tape measure. They are scaffolding, not part of the job — the laser never sees them and they are in no export. Click one with the Select tool to pick it out, then Del — or clear the lot.

Colours

A traced image arrives as dozens of near-identical shades. Slide right to collect them into a few colours you can actually set.

Set every colour to

Outline colour wins over fill. Cut goes through, Score marks the surface, Engrave burns the shaded area away. Need both on one shape — burn the inside, then cut it out? Choose Cut and tick Also engrave the inside.
